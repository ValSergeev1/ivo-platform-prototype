V03 design notes

Emergency is one tap from Home

The Emergency button sends the alert after a 5-second undo ring. No form first. Details and photos are optional afterward.

Read status in 3 seconds

The “Happening now” card shows the one job that needs you, its live stage, and the single next action.

Built for thumbs

44 px minimum targets, 15–17 px body text, a bottom tab bar, and works offline on site.

Invite-only, request first

New customers send a request, then IVO invites them (option 1, approved 2026-11-13).
