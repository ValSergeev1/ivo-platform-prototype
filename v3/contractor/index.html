V03 design notes

One card runs the day

“Today” shows the current job, the window, the checklist and four big actions. No hunting through menus with gloves on.

Camera in the middle

The center tab opens the camera. Photos land on the job timeline with GPS and time.

Punch, markup and voice

Pins on the sheet stay in sync with the list. Mark up a plan with your finger — pen, arrow, shapes, text and punch pins, with undo and saved versions. Voice notes cap at 3 minutes.
