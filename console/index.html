Approval inbox

Everything the system wants to do that affects a customer, a contractor, money or data waits here for a person.

Rule: the system matches contractors by fixed rules, fills in approved templates and flags problems. It never contacts a contractor or customer, awards, prices or changes data until someone with the right role approves it here. Every approval is written to the audit log with the draft and the final version. (AI drafting is Later, outside the launch scope; it would feed this same inbox.)
