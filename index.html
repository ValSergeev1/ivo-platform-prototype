Launch scope (locked)

In: intake, staff console, invite-only customer app and portal, contractor app and vendor portal (application, compliance, job tools), rules-based routing, bid or direct award with your approval before any contractor is contacted, opportunity packets, pre-start document check, job tracking through closeout, change orders against NTE, approval inbox, security, backups and audit.

Later AI bid engine and AI drafting, automated outreach, payments (invoices and payouts), store apps, text messages, public self-serve portal. Where these would appear they're greyed out and marked Later.

Ground rules shown in the design
  • Every action that reaches a customer or contractor, or touches money or data, waits in the Approval inbox for a person.
  • Routing suggestions come from fixed rules (trade, region, valid documents, capacity, scorecard), with the reasons shown.
  • Portal access is invite-only, with email-code sign-in and no passwords.
  • No licensing numbers or company stats in customer-facing screens.
  • Response-time tiers shown are the proposed ones, still to be confirmed.
Decisions for you

1 · How contractors join

A invite-only and staff-vetted · B open application, then verification and a trial job · C referral-based.

Recommended: A, with a closely watched trial first job.

2 · How customers get in

1 request first, then invite · 2 account first (staff-invited) · 3 request plus a tracker link (Later).

Recommended: 1, with 2 for accounts IVO already knows.

3 · Response tiers

Confirm Emergency, Urgent, Standard and Bid targets before the timers go live.

4 · Who approves what

Adjust the role matrix, name a second admin, and decide whether routine template emails (like “crew dispatched”) can be pre-approved.
