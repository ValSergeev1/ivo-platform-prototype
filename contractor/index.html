Design notes

  • Same email-code sign-in as customers. Crew members get their own access from the owner.
  • Every opportunity is a complete packet: scope, trades, files with page counts, deadline with date, time and time zone, questions. IVO can't send an invite if any of these is missing.
  • Exact address only after accepting (or NDA).
  • Questions are answered for every bidder at once.
  • Bids go to IVO, never straight to the customer. A person at IVO approves every award.
  • Check-in uses GPS and time-stamped photos, and works offline.
  • The customer's tracker updates from these actions.
  • Change orders show the effect on the not-to-exceed live. Work beyond NTE needs the customer and IVO to approve.
  • Closeout has a checklist; IVO checks it, then the customer signs off.
  • Expiring documents warn at 30/14/7 days; expired ones pause new work automatically.
  • Payouts are Later (payments are outside the launch scope).
