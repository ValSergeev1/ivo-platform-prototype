Design notes

  • Invite-only: a customer gets in only after IVO reviews their first request (see the onboarding decision).
  • No passwords. A 6-digit email code, with an option to stay signed in on a trusted phone.
  • Approvals sit at the top, so nothing waits on the customer by accident.
  • The red Emergency button is always one tap from home.
  • Progress dots match the stages IVO staff see in the console.
  • Eight short steps: where, trade, scope, urgency, budget/NTE, photos and video, files, review.
  • “Not sure” is always an option; IVO triages it.
  • The NTE becomes a hard limit on the job.
  • Press-and-hold avoids pocket taps.
  • A person calls back; the app shows live stages and emails each change. Text updates are Later (outside launch scope).
  • Response targets are proposed until you confirm them.
  • Customers message IVO, not the contractor. IVO stays accountable.
  • Change orders show the money against the NTE before the customer decides.
  • Closeout = photos, punch list, warranty and lien waiver in one package, then sign-off, then invoice.
  • Company admins invite colleagues and set approval limits.
  • Invoices and payment are Later, outside the launch scope; shown greyed out as a placeholder.
